# Your Account

Open **Account settings** from the account menu (or the sidebar, if you're a member or admin).

## Changing your password

Every signed-in user can change their password under **Account settings → Password**.

![The Account settings page](/images/guide/account.png)

- The **current password is required**.
- After a change, your **other sessions are signed out**.
- Changing your password also invalidates any outstanding [reset link](/admin/password-recovery) for your account.

## Forgotten your password?

There is no email delivery in PrintBench, so the *Forgot password?* page can't send you a link. Ask an admin: they create a private, single-use reset link for you under **Users**. See [Password Recovery](/admin/password-recovery).

## Model-site connections

Members and admins also see connection forms for importing models:

- **MakerWorld**: your account cookie.
- **Thingiverse**: your own App Token.

Credentials are per user, encrypted at rest and never shown again after saving. See [Importing from Model Sites](/guide/imports#connecting-your-accounts).

## Signing out

**Sign out** is in the account menu.

## Joining an instance

You'll get an **invitation link** from an admin (or have an account created for you). Open the link, choose a password and you're in. Invitations can be locked to one email address. See [Users & Roles](/admin/users).
